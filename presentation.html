Problem & Challenge
Pocketful Track Specification

Autonomous Zero-Sum Financial Ledger

Constructing a high-concurrency bitemporal wallet engine without human developers touching business logic.

⚖️

Zero-Sum Conservation

Every transaction must strictly generate balanced debit and credit entries. The global aggregate balance must equal the genesis seed across arbitrary concurrent requests.

⏳

Bitemporal Snapshotting

Strict separation between valid/effective time (when financial state takes effect) and recorded time (when recorded). Corrections append immutable revisions without overwriting history.

🛡️

Race-Condition Isolation

Linearized state mutations with atomic reservation, hold/capture lifecycles, and strict Idempotency-Key deduplication across 100-way concurrent stresses.
